# 🍕 Pizza Sales Report Dashboard

An interactive sales analytics dashboard built to analyze one year (Jan–Dec 2015) of pizza sales data, covering revenue trends, order patterns, and product performance.

## 📊 Overview

This dashboard provides a complete view of pizza sales performance through two main pages:

1. **Home** – High-level KPIs, daily/monthly order trends, and category/size-wise sales breakdown
2. **Best/Worst Seller** – Top and bottom performing pizzas by revenue, quantity, and total orders

## 🎯 Key Metrics (KPIs)

- **Total Revenue: ** 817.86K
- **Average Order Value: ** 38.31
- **Total Pizzas Sold: ** 49,574
- **Total Orders: ** 21,350
- **Average Pizza per Order: ** 2.32

## 🧩 Dashboard Features

### Home Page
- Filters for **Pizza Category** and **Date Range** (01-01-2015 to 31-12-2015)
- **Daily Trend for Total Orders** — bar chart showing order volume by day of week
- **Monthly Trend for Total Orders** — line chart tracking orders across the year
- **% of Sales by Pizza Category** — donut chart (Classic, Supreme, Chicken, Veggie)
- **% of Sales by Pizza Size** — donut chart (Large, Medium, Regular, X-Large, XX-Large)
- **Total Pizza Sold by Category** — bar chart comparison
- **Busiest Days & Times** insight panel — highest orders on Friday/Saturday evenings; peak months are July and January
- **Sales Performance** insight panel — Classic category and Large size drive maximum sales

### Best/Worst Seller Page
- **Top 5 Pizzas** by Revenue, Quantity, and Total Orders
- **Bottom 5 Pizzas** by Revenue, Quantity, and Total Orders
- Helps identify menu items to promote vs. items to reconsider

## 🔑 Key Insights

- Classic category pizzas contribute the most to overall sales and total orders
- Large size pizzas are the best-selling size
- Orders peak on Friday and Saturday evenings
- July and January are the highest order-volume months
- The Thai Chicken and Barbecue pizzas are top revenue generators (~43K each)
- The Brie Carre pizza consistently underperforms across revenue, quantity, and orders
